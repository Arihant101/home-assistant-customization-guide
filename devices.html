19 · Device shopping guide: what's worth buying

🎯 TL;DR: on Docker Desktop, favor devices that talk Wi-Fi/Ethernet (ESPHome, Shelly, Reolink, WLED) or Zigbee through a network coordinator. Skip anything that needs USB, Bluetooth or Thread until you move HA to the UNO Q or a mini PC. On the website you can sort this list by price, setup difficulty, popularity and how common it is, filter by category, and hide what won't work on your setup.

How to read the ratings: - Price: typical US street price seen in Oct–Nov 2026. Prices move, so check before you buy. - Setup: 🟢 plug & play · 🟡 some setup (flashing, an app, adding by IP) · 🔴 tinkerer (wiring, mains power, or flashing firmware). Hire an electrician for anything in your breaker panel. - Popularity: how often r/homeassistant and the HA forum recommend it (★1–5). This is a judgment from community threads and reviews. - Common: how likely you'll find it in an average HA home (●1–5), meaning mass-market reach rather than enthusiasm.
