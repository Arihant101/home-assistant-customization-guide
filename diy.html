🎯 TL;DR: ESP32 boards ($5–10) plus ESPHome let you build almost anything. These are the coolest projects.

Ulanzi TC001 + AWTRIX NG

A ~$50 pixel clock that shows notifications, homelab stats and weather over MQTT. AWTRIX 3 has ended, and its repo now points to the AWTRIX NG rewrite. Matching blueprints.

Room-level presence from your phone or watch using ESPHome Bluetooth proxies. This also fixes Docker Desktop's lack of Bluetooth.

Kindle dashboard

Zigbee2MQTT

Arduino UNO Q ↔ HA via MQTT

Your UNO Q's Linux side runs HA or a Python bridge, and its STM32 microcontroller drives pins or relays. A published tutorial toggles the onboard LED from an HA dashboard.

16 · Wall panels & kiosks

🎯 TL;DR: an old Android tablet + the HA Companion app + Kiosk Mode is the free wall panel. 💸 Fully Kiosk Browser's PLUS license is paid, and WallPanel is the FOSS alternative.

Option Cost Notes
Old tablet + Companion app + Kiosk Mode Free Easiest. Use browser_mod to control it remotely.
WallPanel Free, FOSS Android kiosk app with camera motion wake and MQTT.
Raspberry Pi + Chromium --kiosk Cheap A dedicated screen that boots straight into a dashboard.
Kindle e-ink Free §15.
openHASP ESP32 touchscreen ~$20–40 Fully custom, no tablet needed.
