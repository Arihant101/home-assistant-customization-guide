🔌Marketplace & integrations

Must-install Marketplace projects, plus every integration for your devices and homelab.

04 · Marketplace starter pack

🎯 TL;DR: install these first. Everything else in this guide builds on them.

Mushroom

Clean, rounded cards and chips with a visual editor. The classic first upgrade.

Marketplace

Bubble Card

Pop-ups that slide up from the bottom, sliders and separators, so your dashboard feels like a native app. Huge on Reddit.

Marketplace

card-mod

Apply CSS to any card. Needed for blur, glow and glass effects.

Marketplace

UIX (UI eXtension)

Built on card-mod's ideas, it styles (nearly) any frontend element and adds templating ("Forge") and event wiring ("Broker").

Marketplace

button-card

The most flexible card there is. Behind most "how did they make that?!" dashboards.

Marketplace

layout-card

Grid and masonry layouts with breakpoints for tablet, phone and desktop.

Marketplace

browser_mod

Treat each browser or tablet as a device: pop-ups, navigation and kiosk control.

Marketplace

auto-entities

Cards that fill themselves from filters (for example, "every light that's on").

Marketplace

Spook 👻

Extra actions plus repairs that find broken entity references after you rename things.

Marketplace

Adaptive Lighting

Lights change color temperature with the sun all day. Set it once and forget it.

Marketplace

Powercalc

Estimates power use for devices that have no meter, so your energy dashboard works without smart plugs.

Marketplace

Watchman

Reports missing entities and actions in your YAML, like a linter for your config.

Control HA from Alexa or Google for free (no Nabu Casa)

  • Home-Assistant-Matter-Hub (RiDDiX fork). Publishes HA entities as Matter devices to Alexa, Google Home or Apple Home, locally. The original t0bst4r project ended in January 2026, so use this maintained fork. Docker image: ghcr.io/riddix/home-assistant-matter-hub. ⚠️ Matter needs mDNS, so expect to need option B or C from §02.
  • Emulated Hue (built in): pretends to be a Hue bridge for basic on/off control with Alexa.
  • Manual Alexa Smart Home skill: free, but expect 1–2 hours of AWS console setup.
